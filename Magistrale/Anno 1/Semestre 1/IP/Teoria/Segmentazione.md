[[Edge detection]] [[Istogramma]] [[Regioni]]

• Segmentation separates an image into meaningful regions or
objects.
• Edge-based methods: detect intensity discontinuities and use
them as region boundaries.
• Thresholding methods: classify pixels according to intensity (or
another pixel property).
• Region-based methods: group pixels using similarity and spatial
connectivity.
